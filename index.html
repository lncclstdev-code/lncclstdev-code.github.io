KLSG: Keyed Long-Lasting Smart Gateway

            
A capstone ESP32 smart doorbell that fuses camera pixel deviation, PIR and accelerometer readings into one feature vector and scores it with Mahalanobis distance against a calibrated baseline. Chi-square thresholds at 99.75%, 99.95% and 99.99% confidence grade each anomaly on an RGB LED and a live web dashboard.

            
A copper-pad and spring tamper contact, a fingerprint module and email alerts that name known or unknown visitors complete the system. Accounting for variance and correlation between sensors cut false positives compared with Euclidean distance, and a 3-hour calibration baseline proved the most stable day and night. Against a Ring Doorbell Pro it reached a 100% true positive rate on tampering with a near-zero false positive rate on normal foot traffic. The prototype was assessed at Technology Readiness Level 6 and presented at NEECECON 2026.

ESP-NOW to ESPHome bridge for Home Assistant

            
A protocol bridge that lets low-power ESP devices join a smart home without each one needing a full Wi-Fi connection. The hub receives JSON packets from the devices over ESP-NOW and passes them through UART to an ESPHome node, which exposes the data to Home Assistant dashboards and automations. It also sends commands back to individual devices by MAC address.

            
ESP-NOW devices → bridge → UART → ESPHome → Home Assistant
